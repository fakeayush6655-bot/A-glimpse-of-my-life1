Every poem hides a memory.

Sirf Tera Khayaal....

Some feelings were easier to write than to speak.

Main dil ban kar dhadka,tu aadat nikal gayi,,Meri sachi mohbbat teri fursat nikal gayi....Maine toh tujhe apni hr dua mein rkhaa,,Par teri tawajjo toh sirf zarurat nikal gyii.....Raat bhar jagna mera, sukoon se sona tera,,Bada Mehnga pada mujhe sirf hona tera....Maine toh apni zindagi naam krdi tere,,Par tere liye toh khel tha dil todna mera....Tu bhi wahi hai aur mera haal bhi whi hai,,Is toote hue dil mein tera khayaal wahi hai....Tujhse koi shikwa nahi, bs ye naseeb ki baat hai,,Mere hr ek lafz mein sirf teri hi shuruwaat hai...

— Written from the heart
