Every poem hides a memory.

Sunapan.....

Some feelings were easier to write than to speak.

Khamosiyan hi banti hain ab meri daastan,,Lafzo mein woh pehli si baat na rahi.....Tu paas hokar bhi ek raaz-e-dil hai,Jaise rooh mein ho, par mein na rahi.....Bairan yaadien teri, pehra bithaati hain,,Chura kar sukoon, neendien jalati hain.....Tujhe maanga tha maine ibadat ki tarah,,Pr saza mili aisi jaise kiya ho koi haseen gunah...Tasveer teri ab dhundli si laage,Aaina bhi jaise mujhse khafa ho gaya.....Maangi thi duayein jiske milne ki maine,,Usi sahks ki tanhai meri ek saza ho gaya...Ehsaas tera ek meetha sa dard hai,Ishq mera har mausam mein sard hai....Chupaye nahi chupte zakhm hi itne hain,,Meri khamosiyo mein bikhre, tere naam hi itne hain....

— Written from the heart
