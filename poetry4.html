Ganga aur Ghaat

Some feelings were easier to write than to speak.

Woh behti hui Ganga si Nirmal aur magan,Mein thera hua Banaras ka koi purana ghaat hoon...Woh guzar jati hai mujhse hr roj lipat kr,Mein uske ek jhalak ki pyaas mein din-raat hoon...Mein chahta hoon usse apna muqaddar banana,Magar meri kismat mein sirf intezaar likha hain....Woh kisi aur ki raah ki roshni naa ban jaaye,Bas isi dar ne mera sukun cheen rakha hai....Mere dil ki aarti mein sirf uska hi naam hai,,,Woh meri har ibaadat, mera har kaam hai....Woh abhi kisi ka nhi, bas yhi ek umeed hai,,Warna is ek trfa ishq se drd hi anjaam hai....

— Written from the heart
