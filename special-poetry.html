Not every page is meant for everyone.

Khamosh Baarish, Adhoori Jeet

If you've reached here... perhaps, this poem was waiting for you.

Khidki ke sheeshe par girti ye boonden purani,, Phir likh rahi hain wahi ek adhoori kahani.... Seher ki raunaki toh hr taraf saji hai,, Par dil ke kone mein ek gehri khamosi basi hai... Log milte hai, muskura kar badhai dite hain,, Meri har ek jeet par duayen nayi dite hain.... Hazaro chehre hain jo mujh per nigaah rakhte hain,, Par hum toh kisi ki yaad mein panaah rehte hain... Hath mein fateh hai, par jashn ka mizaaj nahi,, Is shor mein sunne ko koi sachi awaaz nahi..... Baarish to ho rhi hai pr mitti ne mahak khoyi hai,, Jaise meri khusiyan aaj phir akele mein royi hain...... Zindagi chal to rhi hai par rangon se kinara hai,,, Jeet kr bhi lagta hai jaise sb kuch hara hai.... Ye dil be-awaaz bas baarish ki tarah girta rehta hai,, Sab kuch paa kar bhi, kisi ki kami mein jalta rehta hai...

— Some pages are never truly finished.

Thank you for reading the pages of my heart.
