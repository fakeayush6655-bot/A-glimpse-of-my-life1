Every poem hides a memory.

Banaras aur Ishq..

Some feelings were easier to write than to speak.

Woh behti hui Ganga si nirmal, Mein Sadiyon Purana Kinara hun, Woh sabki kismat ka dariya hai, mein bhatka hua ek sitaara hun, Usse chuna to chahta hoon, magar lehro se darr lagta hai, Usse apna banane ki jidd mein, mujhe khud se darr lgta hai.... Mere dil ke pathar pe, naam uska hi khoda hai,, Maine uski ek muskurahat ke liye, apna sukun chroda hai.... Woh shaam ki aarti jaisi hai, jise sab jhuk kr dekhte hain,, Mein andheri gali jaisa, jise koi bhul kr dekhte hain...... Main har subah uske intezaar mein, ghaat pe diya jalata hunn,, Woh mujhse milkar guzarti hai, main raakh bann jata hun,,,... Woh meri rooh ka hissa hai, mein uski raah ki dhool hoon,, Woh ek khilta hua gulshan, mein ek murjhaya phool hoon.......

— Written from the heart
