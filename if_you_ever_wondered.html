CHAPTER I

If You Ever Wondered

kuch baatein jo shayad kabhi kahi nahi gayi

✦
Agar tumne kabhi socha ho ki main itna sab kuch kyun feel karta hoon, toh shayad iska koi ek simple sa answer nahi hai. Mujhe khud nahi pata ki kab ek normal si baat, ek chhoti si conversation aur kisi ka bas apna hona itna important ho gaya. Shayad kuch cheezein decide nahi hoti, bas dheere dheere apni jagah bana leti hain. Mujhe yaad nahi ki kis exact moment par tum mere liye itni important ban gayi. Bas itna yaad hai ki waqt ke saath tumhari presence ordinary nahi rahi. Tumse baat karna, tumhari chhoti chhoti baatein yaad rehna, kisi random cheez ka tumhari yaad dila dena... ye sab kab normal se thoda zyada ho gaya, pata hi nahi chala. Shayad isi wajah se maine kabhi apni feelings ko sirf ek passing phase samajh kar ignore nahi kiya. Main jaanta hoon ki feelings sirf isliye mutual nahi ho jaati kyunki ek insaan unhe dil se feel karta hai. Aur main tumse kabhi ye expect bhi nahi karna chahta ki tum sirf meri feelings ki wajah se kuch feel karo. Bas agar tumne kabhi socha ho ki mere liye ye sab kya tha, toh answer shayad bahut simple hai. It mattered. Tum matter karti ho tum mere life ka ek hissa ban gyi ho. Aur shayad isi liye maine har chhoti cheez ko itni honestly feel kiya. Ismein koi perfect story nahi thi, koi guarantee nahi thi, koi fixed ending nahi hai. Bas ek insaan hai jo tumhe ko genuinely pasand kar baitha, aur phir us feeling ko jhootha banane ki jagah uski respect karna seekhta raha. Shayad tum kabhi meri taraf se is sab ko us nazar se na dekh pao. Aur woh bhi theek hai. Kuch feelings ka hona unke complete hone par depend nahi karta.woh feelings bas exist karti hai Aur yaar ek sawaal hamesha hota h tera ki mein hi kyu ? koi aur ladki nhi kya yaar nhi samajh aata ye sab mujhe aur ek sawaal kyu tumse pyaar h to iska bhi answer nhi hai mere paas par ab in sab chijo se aisa fark padta nhi.
