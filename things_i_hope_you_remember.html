CHAPTER II

Things I Hope You Remember

kuch cheezein jo waqt ke saath bhi kam nahi honi chahiye

✦
Agar kabhi bahut saal baad tum peeche mudkar dekho, toh main nahi chahta ki tumhe sirf bade moments yaad aayein ha pata hai tumhari memory achi nhi hai aur mujhe tum kyu hi yaad rakhna chahogi but. Main chahta hoon ki tumhe woh chhoti chhoti cheezein bhi yaad aayein jo us waqt shayad itni important nahi lagi hongi. Woh random conversations. Woh unnecessary si baatein. Woh moments jinka koi special reason nahi tha, phir bhi pata nahi kyun yaad reh gaye. Kabhi kabhi kisi insaan ki yaad kisi photograph se nahi, ek sentence se aa jaati hai. Kisi song se. Kisi jagah se. Ya bas kisi dhali hui shaam se. Shayad meri sabse favourite cheez ye hai ki tumhari presence ne mujhe ye realise karaya ki memories hamesha bade events se nahi banti. Kabhi kabhi ek normal sa din bhi years baad special lagne lagta hai kyunki us din tumse baat ki hoti hai aree ab to meri tabiyat bhi tumse baat krne par thik hoti hai. Main chahta hoon tum ye bhi yaad rakho ki jo kuch bhi maine feel kiya, woh kisi expectation ke badle nahi tha. Pta h vo chemistry practical vala din was the best day of my life. Main tumse kuch prove karwana nahi chahta tha na kabhi khud ke baare mein kuch prove krna chahta tha. Main bas honest rehna chahta tha. Agar kabhi tumhe lage ki tumne kisi ko hurt kiya, toh shayad is page ko yaad karna aur khud ko blame mat karna. Har feeling ka same answer hona zaroori nahi hota. Har connection ka same destination hona zaroori nahi hota. Saayad mere kinaare ko koi aur kasti apna kinaara banaye. Aur agar kabhi tumhe meri koi baat yaad aaye, toh bas itna yaad rakhna ki ek waqt par koi tha jo tumhari khushi ko genuinely important maanta tha aur vo hamesha maanta rhega tum kabhi bhi aao uske paas vo tumhare liye ussi shiddat se betha hai. Shayad main har cheez ko perfect words mein kabhi explain nahi kar paaya. Shayad kuch jagah par main zyada bol gaya, kuch jagah par bilkul nahi bol paaya. Lekin intention simple thi. Tum meri life ka ek khoobsurat part ho yaar jise mein sirf part nhi balki life banana chahta hoon. Aur main nahi chahta ki time us khoobsurati ko insignificant bana de. Kuch log hame life ke liye kabhi perfect nhi lagte. Phir bhi woh hamari story ko beautiful banane ke liye kaafi hote hain.
