Every poem hides a memory.

Ek Tarfa Izhaar

Some feelings were easier to write than to speak.

Pahaadon ki thandak mein, ek aag dabi hai,,Izhaar ki baat aaj labon pe ruki hai....Sab phool de rahe hain, Sab dil de rhe hain,,Hum apne hi zakhm, khud hi sil de rhe hain...Tumhe khabar tak nahi, hum pr kya beet rhi hain,,Tumhe paana to dur, hum toh tumhe kho-e-rhe hain...Mila nhi ishq toh kya, ye dard bhi haseen hai,,Tum aasman ho mera, aur mein tumhari zameen hoon....Kismat mein likha hoga toh mil hi jaoge,,In vaadiyo ki trh mujh pr khil hi jaoge...Agar keh na saka to bura mt maanna,,Mere is khamosh ishq ko, bas dua jaanna.

— Written from the heart
