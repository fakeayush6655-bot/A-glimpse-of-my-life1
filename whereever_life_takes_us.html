Wherever Life Takes Us

har kahani ko ek hi jagah tak pahunchna zaroori nahi

✦
Shayad life hum dono ko ek hi direction mein le jaaye, shayad bilkul alag directions mein. Shayad aage chal kar humare raste kabhi dobara milenge,ya Saayad hamare raaste bhi ek hi hojaye.... shayad sirf memoriesmein milte rahenge ya Saayad milke memories banaye. Sach bolun toh ab mujhe future ka exact answer jaanne ki utni zaroorat nahi lagti. Maine bahut baar socha hai ki agar cheezein meri marzi ke hisaab se nahi hui toh kya hoga. Pehle lagta tha shayad kisi answer se sab kuch clear ho jayega. Ab lagta hai kuch answers ki zaroorat hi nahi hoti. Main jaanta hoon tum apna mann change nahi karna chahti ho. Aur uske peeche jo bhi reason ho, main us reason ko judge nahi karna chahta. Family ho, circumstances ho, tumhari life mein koi aur ho, ya bas tumhara dil kuch aur chahta ho... ab honestly mujhe in cheezon ke exact answer se farq nahi padta. Kyunki jo tha, woh mere liye kabhi meaningless nahi tha aur na hoga. Tum meri life mein jis tarah aayi, jis tarah kuch moments mere saath banaye, aur jis tarah tumhari presence ne mujhe kuch feelings samajhne di... woh apne aap mein ek bahut khoobsurat tohfa tha. Aur haan, agar kabhi life tumhe mujhse bahut door le jaaye, toh bhi shayad mere andar ek bhaut badi si jagah tumhare naam ki waise hi rahegi. Main intezaar karunga. Na kisi pressure ke saath. Na kisi expectation ke saath. Bas apni jagah se. Ho sakta hai waqt tumhe badal de. Ho sakta hai mujhe sehen hi krne de sirf. Ho sakta hai hum dono bilkul alag log ban jaayein. Ya ho sakta ham dono ek saath ho jaaye... Lekin agar kabhi, kisi bhi point par, tumhe lage ki tumhari kahani ka ek purana panna dobara kholna hai, toh main us panne ko kabhi band hone nahi dunga. Shayad ye thoda foolish lage. Shayad thoda impossible bhi. Lekin kuch cheezein logic se nahi chalti. Main ye nahi jaanta ki kal kya hoga. Main ye bhi nahi jaanta ki hum kahan honge. Bas itna jaanta hoon ki tum meri life ka ek aisa chapter ho jise main kabhi regret nahi karunga. Aur agar life hume kahin bhi le jaaye... main apni jagah se intezaar karta rahunga. Kyunki kabhi kabhi kisi insaan ko paana hi sab kuch nahi hota. Kabhi kabhi uske liye dil mein ek jagah bana paana bhi apne aap mein bahut hota hai. Aur haa mein vase ye bolna nahi chahta par saayad aaj nhi bola to kabhi nhi bol paunga ek din tumne mujhse pucha tha ki aadi bhi tumse meri hi trh pyaar krta h to mein kyu hi pr aaj mein kh rha hu nhi krta vo meri tarah aur naa kabhi kar payega aur naa koi aur us tarah kar payega jese mein krta hu.
