Some feelings were easier to write than to speak.

Sawaalon ki ye gathri, mein kandhon pr uthaya hu,,,Jo mere hai nhi, wo dukh seene mein dabaye hun....Hazaaro raaste hain, pr kadam rukne se drte hain,,,Jo sabki sunte aaye hain, kya khud se baat krte hain...Ujala chehre pr rakh kr, mein sabko muskurata hun,,,,Magar bheed mein aakar, khud ko bhul jaata hun....Zimmedari ki deeware, badi uchi banayi hain,,Ki ab bahar nhi aati, jo andar tanhaai hai....Wo ek kaanta sa chubta hai, dil ke kisi kone mein,,Sukoon milta nhi ab, jaagte ya sone mein....Wahi ek baat ghumti hai, wahi ek pal thaher gaya,,,,Mein upar se to jinda hun, pr andar se bikhar gaya...Sabki mushkile hal ki, sabhi ka haath thama hai,,Magar is dard ka mere, na koi thikaana hai....Mein sabke vaaste haazir, magar khud se hun begana,,Kise maaloom hai andar, ki hoon kitna veerana....Samajh aata nhi mujhko, ye kaisa dard hai dil mein,,Fasa hu khud hi apne hi banaye is muqabil mein....Wo ek inkaar ki thandak, rago mein barf jesi hai,,,Batau kya kisi ko mein, ki ye haalat meri kaisi hai....

— Written from the heart
