Every poem hides a memory.

Khamosh Raaste....

Some feelings were easier to write than to speak.

Har subah ki tarah woh aaj bhi muskuraya,,Aaine ne pucha kuch, toh bas sar jhukhaya....Raaston ko pata thi uske kadmon ki har daastan,,Par kisi mod par khuli na uski zubaan....Hawa ne chhukar phir wahi naam pukaara,,Woh ruka thoda, phir ho gaya tanha dobara....Chai ki thandi pyaali, aur khayalon ka shor,,Baatein haazaar thi, par lafz thode kamzor....Sabne pucha - "kya hai dil ka sawaal?",,Woh hans kar bola - "bas yunhi hai mera haal"....Raaton mein neend usse dhoondhne aati zaroor,,Par uski palkon pe the kuch adhoore dastoor....Kuch khat adhoore, kuch baatein gumnaam,,,,Har panne par likha tha bas wahi anjaam....Bheed mein rehkar bhi vo tanha hi dikha,,Jaise khud se milne ka raasta hi diya ho mita....Na sikwa kisi se, naa koi ilzaam,,,,Bas khamosiyon mein lipta ek beawaaz sa naam...Yunhi chalta raha har din ka silsila,,,,Jaise dil ke seher ko uska humsafar naa mila...

— Written from the heart
