The Part I Never Planned to Write....

Hidden Chapter

Some words were never meant to be spoken aloud...

You weren't supposed to find this... But since you're here, shayad ye bhi padh hi lo. Sach kahu toh mujhe kabhi samajh nahi aaya ki kuch feelings ko words mein kaise explain karte hain. Kuch cheezein aisi hoti hain jo bolne baitho toh lagta hai ki words unhe properly describe hi nahi kar paayenge. Isliye shayad maine unhe kabhi kaha hi nahi... bas feel karta raha. Kabhi kabhi sochta hu ki aakhir aisa kya hai tum mein jo mujhe tumhari chhoti chhoti cheezein tak yaad reh jaati hain. Tumhari koi random si baat, tumhara kisi cheez par hasna, tumhara bina kisi reason ke smile karna... shayad tumhare liye ye sab normal hoga, but pata nahi kyun mere liye nahi hai. Kabhi koi song sunta hu aur suddenly tum yaad aa jaati ho. Kabhi koi random si cheez dekh kar tumhare baare mein sochne lagta hu. Aur kabhi bina kisi reason ke bas tumhara khayal aa jaata hai. Maine kabhi consciously decide nahi kiya tha ki tum meri life ka itna important part banogi. Ye bas dheere dheere hota gaya. Pehle tum ek person thi jisse baat karna achha lagta tha, phir tumhari baatein yaad rehne lagi... phir tumhari presence matter karne lagi... aur pata hi nahi chala kab tumhari absence bhi notice hone lagi. Shayad isi liye maine ye poori si little world banayi. Poems mein woh likha jo directly kehna mushkil tha. Letters mein woh baatein rakhi jo kabhi properly keh nahi paaya. Aur stars mein woh little things chhupa di jo main kabhi bhoolna nahi chahta. Aur ye hidden chapter... ye shayad sabse simple hai. I just want you to know that somewhere along the way, you became someone really special to me. Main nahi jaanta future kya hai, cheezein kahan jaayengi, ya life humein kis direction mein le jayegi. But jo bhi hai, jitna bhi hai, main uske liye genuinely thankful hu. Tum meri story ka koi planned chapter nahi thi. Tum woh chapter ho jo likhte likhte khud important hota gaya. Aur shayad isi liye main tumhe apni story se kabhi write out nahi karna chahta. Some chapters are hidden, not because they are unimportant... but because they are the ones that mean the most. Aur haan... agar kabhi tumhe lage ki tum ordinary ho, toh bas ek baar yahan wapas aa jaana. Shayad ye hidden chapter tumhe yaad dila dega ki kisi ki nazar mein tum kitni special ho. ❤️ — yours, always.
